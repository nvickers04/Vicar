# Core Business Plan — Vicar

**Last updated:** May 4, 2026  
**Phase:** Early planning (docs-only repository)

This is our primary strategic document. All future decisions (product, tech, hiring, partnerships) should be checked against this plan using the `stabilization-pr-checklist.md` and `change-impact-map.md`.

## 1. Vision (in plain English)

Vicar exists to make it radically easier for companies to find, hire, and manage great talent — while treating contractors, employees, and clients fairly and compliantly.

We want to be known for **speed, transparency, and doing things the right way** in an industry that often feels opaque or adversarial.


## 2. Target Customers & Market

**Primary customers (Phase 1):**
- Mid-sized companies (50-500 employees) who need specialized talent but don't want the overhead of a full internal recruiting team
- Specific industries we understand deeply (to be decided — e.g. tech, healthcare, finance, engineering)

**Secondary (later):**
- Large enterprises that use VMS/MSP systems
- Independent contractors looking for better representation

**Market problem we solve:**
Many companies waste months finding the right people or end up with bad fits. Contractors often get low transparency on rates and poor support. We aim to fix both sides of the marketplace.

## 3. Our Approach / Value Proposition

- **For clients:** Faster placements with better vetting, transparent and predictable pricing, strong compliance support, and reduced worry about overtime costs on common lower-wage roles.
- **For talent:** Higher take-home pay where possible, better career support, clear expectations, reliable payment, and protection from excessive overtime on higher-paid roles.
- **Our edge:** Deep process discipline (using this playbook), modern scheduling tools (to be built), and a genuine focus on long-term relationships instead of transactional "body shop" behavior.

**Pricing approach:** We use a simple mathematical banded pricing model with **fixed bill rates per band** (calculated from the band's average pay rate). Details and current rates are maintained in the dedicated `docs/pricing-strategy.md` document. This gives clients predictable invoicing while protecting our margins.

**Business model (how we make money):**
- Primarily through markup on contractor rates (target overall gross margin 34–40%)
- Potential future revenue from premium placement fees, managed services, or software tools we build
- We will track margins rigorously from day one

## 4. First Focus Area (MVP Slice)

We will **not** try to build everything at once.

Proposed first narrow slice (to be validated):
- **Candidate submission tracking + basic client portal**
  OR
- **Time & expense tracking with automated invoicing** (high impact on cash flow and compliance)

We will decide this after customer conversations. This business plan will be updated once we pick one.

**Why narrow?** Small blast radius. Easier to get right. Faster to test assumptions.

## 5. Key Risks & Mitigation (using our impact map)

- **Compliance & tax classification risk** — High priority. We will work with good legal/tax advisors early. This is why we avoid certain words in the company name.
- **Cash flow** — Time between paying contractors and getting paid by clients can kill young companies. Mitigation: tight T&E process and clear payment terms.
- **Talent quality** — Bad placements damage reputation fast. Mitigation: strong screening process and feedback loops.
- **Competition** — Many staffing firms exist. Mitigation: focus on transparency, technology, and specific vertical expertise.

**Hotspots (extra care required):**
- Anything involving money movement, tax withholding, or worker classification
- Candidate data and privacy
- Client contracts and rate agreements

## 6. Success Metrics (how we know we're winning)

- Placement volume and repeat business rate
- Gross margin percentage
- Time-to-fill for roles
- Customer and talent Net Promoter Score (NPS)
- Compliance audit results (zero major findings)
